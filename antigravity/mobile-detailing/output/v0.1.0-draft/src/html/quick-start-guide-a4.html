2. Your 15-Minute Setup Sequence (3 Steps)

Step 1 — Enter Your Real Baseline Costs (7 minutes)

Open job-costing-calculator.xlsx. Edit ONLY the shaded [INPUT] cells: your hourly labor rate, chemical/consumable cost per job (or use Tab 02 for bottle dilutions), round-trip miles & per-mile vehicle rate, and fixed monthly overhead divided by expected monthly jobs.

Step 2 — Set Your Service Menu Prices (5 minutes)

Use Tab 03 (03_Package_Matrix) to test target profit margins across your 3–4 service tiers and vehicle sizes (Sedan, Mid-Size SUV, Large SUV/Truck). Copy your resulting quoted prices onto service-menu.pdf.

Step 3 — Print Clipboard Inspection Sheets (3 minutes)

Print 10–20 copies of vehicle-inspection.pdf (US Letter or A4, 100% / Actual Size, grayscale OK). Keep them on a clipboard in your vehicle and complete a 3-minute walkaround with every customer before touching the paint.

Worked Pricing Example: Margin vs. Markup

Why confusing margin and markup quietly underprices mobile detailing jobs.

PAGE 2 OF 3

The Core Formula Used in job-costing-calculator.xlsx

Total Cost = (Hours × Hourly Rate) + Materials + (Miles × Rate/Mile) + Allocated Overhead
Target Selling Price = Total Cost ÷ (1 − Target Margin)
Quoted Price rounds to the nearest cent ($0.01). Profit = Quoted Price − Total Cost.

Canonical Worked Example: $100.00 Total Job Cost at 40% Target

Suppose a job's real cost is $100.00 ($50.00 labor + $20.00 chemicals + $10.00 travel + $20.00 overhead) and you want a 40% profit margin:

CORRECT: 40% Target Margin

Formula$100.00 ÷ (1 − 0.40)
Unrounded Target Price$166.6666...
Rounded Quoted Price$166.67
Dollar Profit$66.67
Actual Profit Margin40.00%
Equivalent Markup66.67%

COMMON MISTAKE: 40% Markup

Formula$100.00 × (1 + 0.40)
Quoted Price$140.00
Dollar Profit$40.00
Actual Profit Margin28.57%
Shortfall vs. 40% Margin−$26.67 / job
ImpactUnderprices every quote

Rounding & Boundary-Case Behavior

  • Intermediate precision: Labor and travel stay unrounded until Total Cost is summed, avoiding compounding penny drift on fractional hours or miles.
  • Nearest-cent quoting: Recommended Quoted Price uses standard half-away-from-zero rounding to $0.01; Projected Profit uses that rounded quoted price.
  • Zero-revenue / comped jobs: Entering $0.00 as an Actual Selling Price shows exact negative profit and N/A (Zero Revenue) for margin — never a #DIV/0! error.
  • Invalid input protection: Negative numbers, non-numeric entries, or target margins of 100% or higher trigger an explicit ERR: message.

Job-Day Workflow, Printing & Troubleshooting

Practical habits for driveway walkarounds, grayscale printing, and spreadsheet use.

PAGE 3 OF 3

1. Repeatable Job-Day Routine

  • Before Driving: Confirm address, vehicle size tier, and water/power availability; keep blank vehicle-inspection.pdf sheets on a clipboard.
  • On Arrival (3–5 minutes): Walk around the vehicle with the customer. Mark pre-existing scratches, dents, chips, curb rash, stains, or pet hair on the 5-view diagram; confirm final price and collect initials.
  • After Heavy Jobs (2 minutes): Enter actual hours and extra chemical use into 01_Job_Calculator with your quoted price in the Override cell to verify your real margin.

2. Printing & Paper-Size Settings

  • Match your paper size: Use *-us-letter.pdf for US Letter (8.5 × 11 in) or *-a4.pdf for A4 (210 × 297 mm).
  • Scale to 100%: Choose Actual Size / 100% in your print dialog so checkboxes and the 0.78 in clipboard top margin stay accurate.
  • Grayscale-safe design: All forms print cleanly in black-and-white / grayscale with zero heavy dark ink backgrounds.

3. Spreadsheet Compatibility & Troubleshooting

  • Supported apps: Microsoft Excel, LibreOffice Calc, and Google Sheets (File → Import → Upload .xlsx).
  • Entering percentages: Enter 40% (or 0.40) for Target Profit Margin. Entering 100% or higher displays an ERR: banner because 100% margin is mathematically undefined for positive cost.
  • Protected formula cells: Sheets use light, password-free protection to prevent accidental formula overwrites. You may unprotect any sheet in Excel/Calc to customize layout.
  • Security hygiene: Zero VBA macros, zero external workbook links, and zero network calls.
