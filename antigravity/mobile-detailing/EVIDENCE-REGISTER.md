## 3. Protocol for Adding Verified Entries

When owner-authorized research or Codex evidence adapters produce observations:
1. Assign sequential IDs (`EV-001`, `EV-002`, …) and **never overwrite** `EV-HIST-*` entries (preserve audit trail).
2. Populate every column; leave no field blank (use explicit `N/A — reason` only where structurally inapplicable).
3. Separate verbatim quoted passages from inferences so reviewers can audit whether a conclusion actually follows from the quote.
4. Confirm each observation's date is within the **30-day freshness window** before submitting to Studio OS's scoring engine.
5. Never treat engagement counts (views, likes, shop-level review totals) as proof of product-level sales or purchase intent.
