Guest Departure Checklist (5 Minutes Before Leaving)

Load used dishes into the dishwasher (or rinse & place in sink).
Place used bath towels in the designated laundry hamper or tub.
Bag household trash and place it in the outdoor bin (if requested).
Turn off lights, TVs, and set thermostat / AC to eco mode.
Check outlets, closets, and nightstand drawers for chargers & belongings.
Return keys / lockbox and confirm doors are locked; send host a quick checkout message.

Host Turnover & Restock Quick-Check (Internal)

Essentials Restock

  • Fresh linens, pillows, and clean bath/hand towels
  • Toilet paper, hand soap, shampoo & dish tabs
  • Coffee, tea, trash liners & paper towels
  • Verify Wi-Fi router & smart-lock battery level

Damage & Maintenance Check

  • Inspect bedding, rugs, and appliances for issues
  • Reset thermostat, lights, and streaming sign-outs
  • Photograph any damage immediately for records
  • Confirm welcome book & QR card are clean & visible
